About Me

Curiosity, design, and learning.

I am a PhD Candidate in Learning Design and Technology at Purdue University, where I study how people develop Design Thinking through meaningful learning experiences.

My academic and professional experiences span information technology, technology management, educational research, instructional design, and teaching.

01 / My Journey

Academic Background

My journey into learning design began with a background in Information Technology, followed by graduate studies in Technology Management. These experiences shaped my interest in the relationship between technology, people, and learning.

At Purdue University, my doctoral research focuses on understanding the development and assessment of Design Thinking, particularly within computing, engineering, and informal STEM learning environments.

03 / Research Interests

What I Study

My research examines how Design Thinking develops across educational experiences, how these skills can be meaningfully assessed, and how learning environments can support their development.

I am particularly interested in authentic learning experiences, assessment design, problem-solving, and the potential role of human–AI collaboration in learning.

04 / Teaching & Practice

Teaching and Learning Design

Alongside my research, I have designed and facilitated learning experiences for high school and undergraduate learners.

Through Purdue's GERI summer programs, I have taught Think Like an Engineer, creating opportunities for learners to understand users, investigate authentic problems, collaborate, prototype, and reflect on their design decisions.

My instructional experience also includes undergraduate Design Thinking education, workshop design, and educational technology support.

05 / Approach

How I Think About Learning

I am interested in learning environments that encourage curiosity, active exploration, collaboration, and meaningful engagement with real-world problems.

My work brings together the perspectives of a researcher, educator, and learning designer, with an emphasis on connecting educational theory to learning experiences.
