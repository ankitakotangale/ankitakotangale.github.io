About Me

I am a PhD Candidate in Learning Design and Technology in the Department of Curriculum and Instruction at Purdue University.

My Academic Journey

My academic background brings together information technology, technology management, and learning design. I hold a bachelor's degree in Information Technology and a master's degree in Technology Management.

My doctoral studies focus on understanding how design thinking develops in educational settings, particularly in computing and engineering education.

Research Interests

I am interested in design thinking, authentic learning experiences, instructional design, assessment, and the development of problem-solving skills.

My research includes studies with high school learners participating in engineering design activities and students engaged in FIRST Robotics. I am also interested in how human–AI collaboration may shape future design learning experiences.

Teaching and Learning Design

My teaching experience includes facilitating engineering design activities through Purdue's gifted education programs. I enjoy creating opportunities for learners to collaborate, reflect, test ideas, and engage with authentic problems.

Beyond Research

I value curiosity, thoughtful collaboration, and creating learning experiences that connect ideas with real-world practice.
