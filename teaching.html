02 / Learning Design

Learning Design & Curriculum Development

I am interested in designing learning environments where learners engage with meaningful problems, investigate user needs, test possible solutions, and reflect on their learning.

Authentic Design Challenges

Learning activities centered on realistic engineering and design problems, including classroom redesign and prototype development.

Human-Centered Design

Activities that help learners understand user perspectives through empathy mapping, problem definition, and user feedback.

Collaborative Learning

Structured opportunities for teamwork, communication, idea generation, and collaborative problem-solving.

Reflection & Iteration

Learning experiences that encourage students to revisit design decisions, refine prototypes, and reflect on feedback.

03 / Philosophy

My Approach to Teaching

I approach teaching as an opportunity to create meaningful experiences where learners actively investigate problems, collaborate with others, and develop thoughtful solutions.

I value learning environments that support curiosity, creative exploration, reflection, and the practical application of knowledge.
