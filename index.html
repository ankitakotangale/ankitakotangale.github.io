01 / About

Learning through design

I study how people learn to understand problems, collaborate with others, and create thoughtful solutions. My interests connect design thinking, instructional design, assessment, and authentic learning experiences.

At Purdue University, I investigate the development of design thinking among learners in computing and engineering contexts.

02 / Research

Areas of inquiry

My research examines how design thinking can be developed, taught, and assessed.

Design Thinking Development

Understanding how learners develop design thinking mindsets through authentic experiences.

Instruction & Assessment

Exploring instructional practices and methods for assessing design thinking.

Computing & Engineering Education

Investigating design thinking in computing, engineering, and robotics contexts.

Human–AI Collaboration

Exploring how AI-supported approaches may influence design learning and human agency.

03 / Teaching

Designing learning experiences

My teaching focuses on creative problem-solving, collaboration, reflection, and learning through hands-on design challenges.

I have taught engineering-centered activities through Purdue's gifted education summer programs.
