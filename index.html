Researching how people learn, think, and design.

About Me

At the intersection of design and learning

My academic background connects information technology, technology management, and learning design. I am particularly interested in how learners develop the confidence, skills, and mindsets needed to approach complex problems.

Through my research and teaching, I explore design-centered learning in computing, engineering, and informal STEM environments.
