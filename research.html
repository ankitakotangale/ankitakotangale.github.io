Research & Scholarship

My Research

I investigate how design thinking develops through authentic learning experiences, and how educators can support and assess these skills in computing and engineering education.

Research Project 01

Design Thinking in Computing and Engineering Education

Higher Education Research

This study explores computing and engineering educators' perspectives on the role of design thinking in professional practice, including the skills they consider important and the ways these skills can be incorporated into education.

Design Thinking Faculty Perspectives Higher Education
Research Project 02

Developing Design Thinking Through Authentic Learning

GERI High School Study

This study examines students' development of design thinking mindsets and skills during hands-on engineering design experiences in Purdue's gifted education summer programs. It uses pre- and post-assessments to explore changes in learners' design thinking.

Authentic Learning Engineering Design Assessment
Research Project 03

Design Thinking in FIRST Robotics

Robotics Education Research

This research investigates design thinking in FIRST Robotics learning environments, including the experiences of students participating in FTC and FRC programs. It considers how collaborative engineering design experiences relate to design thinking.

FIRST Robotics FTC & FRC Collaborative Design
Future Research

Proposed Research Direction

My future research interests include examining how human–AI collaboration may influence design thinking processes, learner agency, and the development of design solutions.
